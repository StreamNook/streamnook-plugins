# Autopilot

Tracks drops and channel-point progress on your followed channels in the background.

- **Drops:** click a drop in the Drops center and it runs that one for you, no stream window needed. Progress shows live in the title bar and the Drops center; Mine All runs through your campaigns by priority.
- **Channel points:** off by default. Turn it on to collect points across your other followed live channels.

Bonus chests and finished drops are claimed automatically while it runs. Priority channels, preferred and excluded games, and recovery options live on its settings panel.

It asks for your Twitch login once, so it can report watch time and claim rewards for you.
