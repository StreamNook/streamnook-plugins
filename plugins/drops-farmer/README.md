# Autopilot

Earn Twitch drops and channel points without keeping a stream open.

- **Drops:** click a drop in the Drops center and it mines that one for you, no stream needed. Progress shows live in the title bar and the Drops center; Mine All runs through your campaigns by priority.
- **Channel points:** off by default. Turn it on to farm points across your other followed live channels.

Bonus chests and finished drops are claimed automatically while it runs. Priority channels, preferred and excluded games, and recovery options live on its settings panel.

It asks for your Twitch login once, to report watch time and claim for you. Everything it does, you could do yourself by watching.
